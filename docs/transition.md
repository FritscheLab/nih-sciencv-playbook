# Department workflows

Operational guidance for departments and grant teams supporting repeatable SciENcv Common Forms preparation across a portfolio of NIH submissions.

Use this operating sequence:

1. Maintain [identity, access, and reusable-data readiness]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link getting-ready.md %}).
2. Determine the required people and documents from the NOFO and [submission stage]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link other-support/lifecycle-submission.md %}).
3. Support preparation of the applicable [biosketch]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link biosketch.md %}) and [CPOS]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link other-support.md %}) documents.
4. Complete content and compliance checks before the handoff.
5. Reserve a window for each named individual to review and certify.
6. Deliver the certified, unmodified Common Forms to the submission owner.

Use [submission planning and tracking]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link transition/department-plan.md %}) for a single deadline and [portfolio readiness]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link transition/bulk-migration.md %}) across multiple labs or submissions.
