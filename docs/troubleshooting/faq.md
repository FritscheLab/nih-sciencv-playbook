# FAQ

## Can my admin certify for me?

The person named on the form must personally certify from their own SciENcv account. Keep your password, verification codes, and signed-in session private. Staff can help prepare the form through [SciENcv delegation]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link getting-ready/delegates.md %}).

## Can I edit the PDF after I download it?

Keep each certified SciENcv PDF intact to preserve its machine-readable metadata. Follow the NIH Application Guide or notice of funding opportunity (NOFO) if it expressly requires a special attachment such as a flattened compilation of participating-faculty biosketches. When NIH requests foreign contracts or separate MFTRP statements, upload those as separate flattened attachments.

## Do I always submit CPOS with an NIH application?

No. For many NIH applications, current and pending support is requested later in the process (often during **JIT**) or in **RPPR/Prior Approval** workflows. At application stage, follow the **NOFO** and the **NIH Application Guide**. One important application-stage exception is **mentored career development** applications, which require CPOS for **mentor/co-mentor(s)**, not for the candidate.

## Where do I put citations for my Personal Statement and Contributions?

Do **not** add a full bibliographic references list or hyperlinks in the NIH Supplement narrative boxes. Either narrative may refer parenthetically to any selected Common Form Product. NIH suggests lead author/year or PMID/PMCID.

## Is Research Security Training the same as NIH’s Other Support disclosure training?

No. Each senior/key person must complete qualifying RST within the 12 months before application submission and certify through the biosketch; the AOR provides the institutional certification for covered individuals employed by the applicant organization. NIH also requires recipients to train senior/key personnel on institutional Other Support disclosure policies.

## Where does the annual MFTRP certification go?

When you need a separate malign foreign talent recruitment program (MFTRP) statement, upload it in Section G.1 of the Research Performance Progress Report (RPPR) as `MFTRPcert_[Name].pdf`. Check [Research-security certifications]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link policy/research-security-certifications.md %}) to determine when to provide that statement and how to sign and submit it.

## Can I reuse an older certified PDF?

Check that the content is current, the certification/signature date is within **12 months** of submission, and the PDF passes submission validation. The [September 2026 signing-certificate alert]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link troubleshooting/september-2026-certificates.md %}) can affect PDFs even within that window. If an update is needed, re-certify in SciENcv, download a fresh PDF, and replace the attachment. Renaming the file is fine; editing the PDF content is not.

## Do I report institutional core facilities or shared equipment in CPOS?

Usually **no**. Broadly available institutional core facilities and shared equipment belong under **Facilities and Other Resources**, not Other Support / CPOS.

## Do I report an in-kind contribution if there is no associated time commitment?

No. NIH says an in-kind contribution does **not** need to be reported when there is **no associated commitment of the individual’s time**.
