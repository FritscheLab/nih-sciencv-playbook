# Transition from the old Other Support format

- The legacy NIH Other Support format remains required for submissions on/before **Jan 24, 2026**.
- For submissions on/after **Jan 25, 2026**, use the SciENcv CPOS Common Form.

Use your prior Other Support document as a source, but rebuild it in SciENcv and certify.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    accTitle: Legacy Other Support transition
    accDescr: Legacy Other Support remains a source document, but submissions on or after January 25, 2026 use SciENcv CPOS and individual certification.
    A["Legacy Other Support source"] --> B["Extract projects, support, and in-kind items"]
    B --> C["Rebuild in SciENcv CPOS"]
    C --> D["Resolve missing fields"]
    D --> E["Named individual certifies"]
    E --> F["Certified CPOS PDF"]
```
