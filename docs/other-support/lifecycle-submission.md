# Submission lifecycle: application, JIT, RPPR, and Prior Approval

Do not turn “Common Forms apply” into “attach both forms for everyone.” The required document depends on the **person, role, mechanism, submission stage, and current NIH instructions**.

| Stage | Operational rule |
|---|---|
| Application | Attach the combined Biosketch Common Form + NIH Supplement for every individual listed on the R&R Senior/Key Person Profile (Expanded), including OSCs. NIH generally does not collect CPOS with the initial application under its JIT policy; follow any mechanism-specific exception. |
| JIT | Submit only when NIH specifically requests JIT information. Use the person-level fields for the applicable biosketch, CPOS, and foreign supporting documentation. |
| Annual RPPR | Use Section D person-level fields when a participant’s role or support status triggers an attachment. Check the MFTRP evidence rules below. |
| Final / Interim RPPR | Follow the final/interim instructions below; the annual participant-change attachment matrix does not apply. |
| Prior Approval | Use the eRA Commons Prior Approval Module and follow the specific request type. A Change of PD/PI request has defined person-level biosketch and CPOS requirements. |

## Application

- Attach a combined, individually certified biosketch PDF for **every individual listed on the R&R Senior/Key Person Profile (Expanded)**, including every **Other Significant Contributor (OSC)**. See [NIH Common Forms FAQ 57968](https://grants.nih.gov/faqs#/common-forms-biographical-sketch-current-pending-support.htm?anchor=57968) and [FAQ 57969](https://grants.nih.gov/faqs#/common-forms-biographical-sketch-current-pending-support.htm?anchor=57969).
- CPOS is usually collected later, often at JIT, rather than for every person at initial submission.
- Follow the NOFO and Application Guide for exceptions. For example, mentored career development applications require CPOS for mentor/co-mentor(s), not for the candidate.
- Participating-faculty biosketches for the PHS 398 Research Training Program Plan are individually completed and certified in SciENcv, then combined and flattened for that specific attachment.

## Just-in-Time (JIT)

Submit JIT information **only when NIH specifically requests it**. In eRA Commons:

- JIT authority cannot be delegated.
- The PD/PI can upload and save JIT information.
- Only a Signing Official (SO) can submit it to NIH.
- Common Forms and foreign supporting documents use separate person-level fields.

Keep each SciENcv Common Form unmodified. When required for a reported foreign appointment or employment, flatten the contract copy and attach it separately in **Foreign Contract CPS**. Each JIT upload field accepts one PDF up to **6 MB**. [eRA JIT help](https://www.era.nih.gov/erahelp/commons/commons/status/jit.htm).

## Annual RPPR Section D: participant changes

Use this decision matrix with the current RPPR instructions:

| Participant condition | Biosketch | CPOS | Foreign supporting documentation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| New senior/key person | Required | Required | If applicable |
| Existing senior/key person with new or changed active support | No, unless otherwise requested | Required | If applicable |
| New Other Significant Contributor | Required | Not required | Not required |
| New training faculty member reported in a Training RPPR | Required | Required | If applicable |

The last row is a narrow Training RPPR reporting rule. It does not convert all program directors, training faculty, or people involved in training-grant oversight into routine CPOS submitters.

For a new senior/key person whose biosketch was already provided, check with your Signing Official before reusing or omitting the attachment. The RPPR guide shows a required upload for new senior/key personnel, while NIH’s FAQ limits that requirement to people for whom a biosketch has yet to be provided. The guide also specifies a biosketch for new OSCs. [NIH RPPR Instruction Guide, Section D.1](https://grants.nih.gov/sites/default/files/rppr_instruction_guide.pdf), [FAQ 57968](https://grants.nih.gov/faqs#/common-forms-biographical-sketch-current-pending-support.htm?anchor=57968).

For changed active support, the CPOS requirement applies to senior/key personnel **regardless of effort**, including less than 1.0 person-month ([FAQ 57908](https://grants.nih.gov/faqs#/common-forms-biographical-sketch-current-pending-support.htm?anchor=57908)).

Check what evidence to provide for [RPPR MFTRP certification]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link policy/research-security-certifications.md %}#rppr-certification-evidence).

## Final and Interim RPPRs

[NIH FAQ 57904](https://grants.nih.gov/faqs#/common-forms-biographical-sketch-current-pending-support.htm?anchor=57904) directs recipients to answer **No** to the D.1 questions about changed CPOS, new senior/key personnel, and new OSCs in final/interim reports. Those reports do not require updated Common Forms for those changes. MFTRP certification still applies; follow the [certification guidance]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link policy/research-security-certifications.md %}).

## Prior Approval

All Prior Approval requests for NIH grants and cooperative agreements must be made through the **eRA Commons Prior Approval Module**. A Signing Official (SO) must initiate and submit every request. The module includes named request types and an **Other Request** route; use the documentation appropriate to the action and upload PDFs as instructed.

### Change of PD/PI

For the eRA Change of PD/PI request:

- only an SO can initiate and submit the request;
- proposed PD/PI effort may not be zero;
- attach one certified biosketch and one certified CPOS PDF for each proposed PD/PI; and
- add foreign supporting documentation separately when applicable.

Other Prior Approval request types can have different fields and evidence. Follow the specific module, Notice of Award, NOFO, and current NIH instructions. When an Other Request adds senior/key personnel, provide applicable current biosketch and CPOS Common Forms as supporting documents.

## PDF handling by attachment type

| Attachment | Handling |
|---|---|
| SciENcv biosketch or CPOS Common Form | Rename if needed, but otherwise keep the certified PDF unmodified unless NIH instructions expressly say otherwise |
| Foreign appointment/employment contract copy | Flatten and upload separately in the designated supporting-documentation field |
| Participating-faculty biosketch attachment | Combine individually certified forms and flatten the compiled attachment as NIH instructs |
| Separate MFTRP RPPR statement, when needed | Flatten the signed statement PDF and upload it in Section G.1 |
